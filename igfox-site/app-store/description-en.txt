IGFox is an Instagram carousel maker for iPhone. Palettes and fonts are already on the slide. You change the text, photos, and look.

The editor is on the phone. Open a slide, swap the copy, pick a palette and a font, fit the canvas to a 1080×1440 carousel or a 1080×1080 post. You do not need a separate design file: the composition is already there, you drop in the content.

Color schemes and character fonts are chosen for the feed, so the first slide has a clear voice. Apply the look to every slide in one tap. Save to the IGFox album in Photos, or share. No watermark.

People use it for beauty and skincare, fitness, finance, coaches, food, and travel.

IGFox is for you if:
• you run a blog and want carousels people save and swipe through to the end
• you handle several clients and need a distinct look for each
• there is no designer on staff, but the feed still needs to look considered

Handmade carousels stay free: unlimited projects, up to 15 slides, both canvas sizes, text markup, photos, and watermark-free export.
